Frequently Asked

Everything About Our Decants

What is a fragrance decant?

A decant is a genuine, authentic fragrance transferred from the original designer bottle into a sterile, high-grade glass atomizer sprayer (2ml, 5ml, or 10ml) so you can test or wear it without purchasing an expensive full bottle.

Are your fragrances 100% authentic?

Yes, absolutely. We purchase all full-size bottles directly from authorized luxury department stores and verified boutique distributors. We never sell clones, dupes, or diluted juices.

How many sprays are in each decant size?

A 2ml sample yields approximately 30-35 sprays; a 5ml travel atomizer yields about 75-80 sprays; and a 10ml deluxe atomizer yields roughly 150+ sprays.
